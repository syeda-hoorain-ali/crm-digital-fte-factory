# CloudStream Pricing Plans

CloudStream offers three pricing tiers designed to meet the needs of teams of all sizes, from freelancers to large agencies.

## Plan Comparison

### Starter Plan - $19/month

Perfect for freelancers and small teams just getting started.

**Features:**
- Up to 5 active projects
- SmartBoards with Kanban view
- TimeStream time tracking
- Basic ClientBridge portal
- Email support only
- No API access
- No custom branding

**Best For:** Freelancers, solo consultants, small teams with limited projects

### Pro Plan - $49/month

Our most popular plan for growing agencies and teams.

**Features:**
- Unlimited active projects
- SmartBoards with Kanban and Gantt charts
- TimeStream with Chrome extension
- Full ClientBridge portal with white-labeling
- Custom branding (logo and colors)
- API access for integrations
- Email and WhatsApp support

**Best For:** Growing agencies, established teams, businesses needing custom branding

### Enterprise Plan - Custom Pricing

Tailored solutions for large organizations with specific needs.

**Features:**
- Everything in Pro, plus:
- Custom domain for client portal
- 24/7 phone support with SLA
- Dedicated account manager
- Advanced security features
- Custom integrations
- On-premise deployment options
- Training and onboarding assistance

**Best For:** Large agencies, enterprises, organizations with compliance requirements

## Feature Matrix

| Feature | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |

## How to Upgrade

To upgrade your plan:
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Change Plan"
3. Select your desired plan
4. Confirm payment details
5. Changes take effect immediately

**Note:** When upgrading, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle.
