# Billing and Invoice Troubleshooting

This guide covers common billing and payment issues for CloudStream subscriptions.

## Updating Payment Methods

### How to Update Your Credit Card

If you need to update your payment information:

**Steps:**
1. Log in to your CloudStream account
2. Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods
3. Click "Add Payment Method"
4. Enter your new credit card details
5. Click "Save"
6. Set the new card as default (if desired)
7. Optionally, remove the old payment method

**Supported Payment Methods:**
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Debit cards
- Apple Pay (on supported devices)
- Google Pay (on supported devices)

### Failed Payment

If your payment fails:

**Common Reasons:**
- Insufficient funds
- Expired credit card
- Card declined by bank
- Incorrect billing address

**What Happens:**
1. You'll receive an email notification about the failed payment
2. CloudStream will retry the payment automatically after 3 days
3. If payment fails again, another retry occurs after 7 days
4. After 3 failed attempts, your account will be suspended

**To Resolve:**
- Update your payment method immediately
- Contact your bank if the card should be working
- Click "Retry Payment" in Settings > Billing

## Invoices and Receipts

### Accessing Invoices

**View All Invoices:**
1. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoice History
2. Click on any invoice to view details
3. Click "Download PDF" to save a copy

**Email Receipts:** CloudStream automatically emails a receipt to your account email after each successful payment.

### Invoice Questions

**Incorrect Amount:** If you believe you were charged incorrectly, check:
- Whether you upgraded/downgraded mid-cycle (prorated charges)
- If you added additional users or features
- Your current plan and billing cycle

**Missing Invoice:** If you made a payment but didn't receive an invoice:
- Check your spam folder
- Verify the email address in Settings > Account > Email
- Download from Settings > Billing > Invoice History

## Refund Policy

CloudStream has a customer-friendly refund policy designed to ensure satisfaction.

### 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

**New Subscriptions:** CloudStream offers a 14-day "No Questions Asked" refund for new subscriptions.

**Eligibility:**
- First-time subscribers to any paid plan
- Request must be made within 14 days of initial payment
- Applies to monthly and annual subscriptions

**How to Request:**
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Request Refund"
3. Confirm your request
4. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days

### Refunds After 14 Days

**Case-by-Case Basis:** Refund requests beyond the 14-day window are handled individually by human support agents.

**Factors Considered:**
- Reason for refund request
- Account usage and activity
- Length of subscription
- Previous refund history

**How to Request:** Contact support at support@cloudstream.com with:
- Your account email
- Reason for refund request
- Any relevant details about your experience

**Response Time:** Human agents will review your request and respond within 2-3 business days.

### Automatic Refunds

**Billing Errors:** If CloudStream makes a billing error (duplicate charge, wrong amount, etc.), refunds are processed automatically once identified.

**Downgrade Credits:** When downgrading plans, unused time is credited to your account and applied to future invoices.
