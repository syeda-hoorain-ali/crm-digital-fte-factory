# ClientBridge Portal Module

ClientBridge is CloudStream's client-facing portal that enables seamless collaboration between your team and your clients.

## White-Labeling

Make the client portal truly yours with custom branding options.

**Available Customizations:**
- Upload your company logo
- Set custom brand colors (primary and accent)
- Customize email templates with your branding
- Add custom domain (Enterprise plan only)

**Availability:** White-labeling features are available on the **Pro Plan** and above.

**How to Configure:**
1. Go to Settings > Branding
2. Upload your logo (PNG or SVG, max 2MB)
3. Select your brand colors using the color picker
4. Preview changes before publishing
5. Click "Save and Apply"

## Client Approvals

ClientBridge includes a powerful approval system that streamlines the review and feedback process.

### Sticky Note Comments

Clients can leave "Sticky Note" style comments directly on design files, making feedback clear and actionable.

**Supported File Types:**
- PDF documents
- PNG images
- JPG/JPEG images

**How It Works:**
1. Upload a design file to the project
2. Share the approval link with your client
3. Client clicks on the file to place sticky notes
4. Each note can include text feedback and be marked as resolved
5. Team receives notifications for new comments

**Benefits:**
- Visual feedback directly on designs
- No more confusing email threads
- Clear tracking of what's been addressed
- Faster approval cycles
